Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use them while charging?

Yes, the Macklor M1 supports high-res audio playback directly through the included USB-C cable, allowing you to listen losslessly while the device charges.

When will my pre-order ship?

Initial waitlist members will secure a spot in our first manufacturing batch. We expect to begin fulfilling pre-orders in Q1 2027.

Do they support multi-point connection?

Absolutely. Thanks to Bluetooth 5.3, you can seamlessly connect to your laptop and smartphone simultaneously, auto-switching when a call comes in.
